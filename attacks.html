Common Cyber Attacks

Cyber attacks are attempts to access, damage or misuse digital systems and information.

1. Phishing

Fake emails or messages designed to trick people into sharing personal information.

2. Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices or access information without permission.

3. Ransomware

A type of malware that locks access to files and demands payment.

4. Identity Theft

Using another person's personal information without permission.

5. Password Attacks

Attempts to gain unauthorized access to accounts by guessing or stealing passwords.

6. Social Engineering

Tricking people into revealing confidential information or taking unsafe actions.
