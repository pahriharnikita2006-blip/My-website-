About Cyber Security

Cyber Security is the practice of protecting computers, mobile devices, networks and data from digital attacks.

Why is Cyber Security Important?

  • Protects personal information.
  • Prevents unauthorized access.
  • Keeps online accounts secure.
  • Protects devices from harmful software.
  • Helps people use the internet safely.

Our Mission

CyberSafe aims to spread awareness about online safety and help people understand common cyber threats.
