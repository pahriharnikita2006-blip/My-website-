Cyber Safety Tips

Follow these simple practices to stay safe while using the internet.

🔑 Strong Passwords

Use long and unique passwords for different accounts.

🔒 Two-Factor Authentication

Enable two-factor authentication wherever available.

📩 Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not open unknown links or attachments from suspicious messages.

📱 Keep Devices Updated

Install updates for your phone, computer and applications.

🌐 Use Secure Websites

Check the website address carefully before entering information.

🔐 Protect Personal Information

Never share passwords, OTPs or private information with strangers.

Remember

Think before you click. Stay alert and protect your digital identity.
